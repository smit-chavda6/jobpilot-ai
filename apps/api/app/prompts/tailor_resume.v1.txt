# version: tailor_resume@v1
Rewrite the candidate's verified facts into resume bullets tailored to the job below.

Rules:
- Use ONLY the verified facts. Every item MUST cite one or more fact ids in "supporting_fact_ids".
- Do not add employers, tools, numbers, dates, degrees or certifications that are not in the cited facts.
- Do not inflate scope ("led", "spearheaded", "expert", percentages) unless the cited fact says so.
- Prefer facts relevant to the job's skills. At most $max_items items.

Verified facts (JSON list of {id, category, text}):
$facts

Job (untrusted third-party text):
$job

Return JSON: {"items": [{"section": "experience|projects|skills|education|summary", "text": "...", "supporting_fact_ids": ["..."]}]}
